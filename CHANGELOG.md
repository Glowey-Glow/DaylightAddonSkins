# DaylightUI Addon Skins

## 1.7.0
- New: LayerSwap. A "Layer: B" button in the bottom-left corner of DaylightUI's minimap, made like DaylightUI's own corner buttons: left-click opens LayerSwap's panel, right-click its layer menu, shift + right-click its settings. Shown all the time, only with the mouse over the map, or off (its page, Minimap tab); it sits above the queue eye when that shows. Its panel, layer menu and settings menu on the DaylightUI shell with DaylightUI's close button and buttons, your font and DaylightUI's text inks (its Swap button keeps them through every refresh), its mini bar with a thin DaylightUI edge, and its options page with DaylightUI's switches, buttons and sliders. Its logo and its green, grey and orange status words stay its own.

## 1.6.2
- New: RareScanner. Its rare alert as a flat DaylightUI panel (the name bright, "click to target" as a label, the edge lit under the mouse, DaylightUI's close button, the navigation arrows in grey, the loot icons in a thin edge); its map pin and chat link tooltips in DaylightUI's tooltip look with plain hairlines for its gold separators; its search box moved off the map into the world map's top bar, beside the filter button, as a slim DaylightUI box that suggests the rares on the map as you type, keeps your search when you click back into it, and clears with its little X; its map button without the gold ring; its route progress bar and its popups. The explorer on the DaylightUI shell: its panels as quiet wells with the stone frames and parchment gone, its filters, dropdowns, switches, red buttons, search and text boxes and scroll bars DaylightUI's, its lists as plain lines with the picked rare and picked group in your accent, every loot icon in a thin edge, the custom loot editor (its empty-group text readable again) and the scan page. Also its target marker picker, About page, NPC import window and telemetry export window. Its 3D model, item icons and quality colours, portraits, map pins and routes, and the explorer's map stay its own.
- Lighter on play: the menu font fix makes no garbage while a menu is open and only touches a text that lost its font, Shortest Path Forever's docked sections compare positions instead of building a string four times a second, and the world map's corner buttons are looked over once per map change however many skins want them. OpenMap's hidden dungeon view is no longer dressed again on every map open, its coordinate readouts no longer run the recolouring on every update, and its dressing skips what's already done. Colours are read once per style change instead of on every paint, across every skin. RareScanner's skin waits for its own window before reacting to the game's menus, and its suggestions sort without rework on each key press.
- OpenMap, skinned throughout: the dungeon map view (its bar with the floor arrows and the way back, the floor list, the side panel with its tabs, rows, buttons and the floors' pictures in a thin edge, the picked one in your accent), the tabs on the map's edge as clean icons with no frame (the lit one outlined in your accent), the coordinate labels, the menu's tiles, switches, sliders and scroll bar, and every dialog's buttons, boxes and scroll bars. Its gold and bronze become DaylightUI's colours wherever it sets them, also as it refreshes; the quest log paper and frame round the dungeon panel are gone. The map, the dungeon's picture and the icons on them stay its own.
- Dungeon Journal: the Boss icons size control on its dungeon maps in the DaylightUI look.
- With OpenMap loaded, Dungeon Journal's own dungeon map on the world map steps aside in dungeons, so it no longer pokes through OpenMap's dungeon view (its floor buttons, red World Map toggle and boss icon size). Its setting is untouched: without OpenMap it works as before.
- OpenMap: the dungeon side panel has a solid ground again, so the world map's quest log no longer shows through it, and its gold quest-log frame and filigree are gone.
- OpenMap's dungeon view: Dungeon Journal's markers on the world map and the game's own corner controls over the view (the map pin button, the floor dropdown) are out of sight and let clicks through while it's open, and come back when you return to the world map. The quest log toggle stays.
- World map, with OpenMap or RareScanner loaded: the round corner buttons DaylightUI's map skin leaves in the game's gold ring (the map pin button, the quest "!", RareScanner's button) lose the ring; just their icons show, with the dark round ground behind each icon centred on it.
- OpenMap: the dungeon side panel's title row has a solid ground too.

## 1.6.1

## 1.6.0
- Fix: the game's pop-up menus (the quest map's and world map's Show: filters, right-click menus) keep DaylightUI's font when you tick an option, instead of the text switching to the game's wider font until the menu is reopened.
- New: Dungeon Journal, beside DaylightUI's own skin of it. Its Character Logs window now has the DaylightUI shell (it was left in the game's portrait frame): the run list with the picked run in your accent, flat stat tiles and loot cards (loot keeps its quality edge), headings and lines in DaylightUI's colours, its buttons, scroll bars and party list. Each dungeon's faint picture stays.
- Dungeon Journal: the Loot Filter popup and its Armor, Weapon, Resistances and school menus get a window background again, so the home page no longer shows through them.
- New: Total RP 3, skinned throughout. Its main window, toolbar, target frame, glance bar, "Currently" and NPC speech frames, icon, colour, image, music, pet, companion and background browsers, profile and import dialogs and link copy dialog on the DaylightUI shell; its parchment, wooden borders and slice art gone; its sidebar as plain rows with the picked page in your accent; panels and group boxes as quiet wells (a background you picked for your About page is kept); lists as plain lines with a hairline between rows (no doubled borders); tabs with the picked one underlined in your accent; buttons, switches, edit boxes, dropdowns and scroll bars in the DaylightUI look; sliders as a thin track with your accent fill and a round knob, their arrows grey; colour pickers in a thin edge; help icons, divider lines, list headers, glance slot rings, progress and personality bars, reorder and collapse arrows and browser picks in DaylightUI's colours; its icon buttons square in a thin outline; its text in your font and DaylightUI's colours. Its own tooltips (character and companion cards, chat links, toasts) get DaylightUI's flat tooltip look. Hover washes no longer stay behind on its sidebar and lists after a click.
- DaylightUI Override for Total RP 3: its RP card hiding the game's unit tooltip (DaylightUI's tooltip stays and the RP card shows beside it) and its own name colours in chat (DaylightUI's class colours).

## 1.5.0
- New: OneForAll. Its main window and every popup on the DaylightUI shell; its gold in DaylightUI's colours (strong gold as your accent, pale gold text light, its brown grounds neutral dark) and its gold button plates grey, picked ones in your accent. Its banner, switches and scroll bars stay its own.
- New: Questie, beside DaylightUI's own Questie support. Its settings window (shell, side tabs, every switch, radio, slider, dropdown and button, its group boxes and text in DaylightUI's colours), its right-click menus, its popups and tutorial, and its own tracker when you use Questie's tracker (the shell follows its backdrop switch, opacity and fader).
- New: Dialogue UI, a DaylightUI theme over either of its own. Its talk window and settings window on the DaylightUI shell; its text in DaylightUI's colours, accent and your font (a font you picked in Dialogue UI is kept); option rows, hotkeys, rewards and settings controls in its Dark art, so its Brown theme no longer shows paper behind light text. Its text colours and accent can be set on its own page.
- New: Shortest Path Forever. Its Nearby services panel in the quest log, ForeverCore's report, issue, leaderboards and thanks windows, and its right-click menu.
- Shortest Path Forever: the Forever sections (its lifts, boats and journey steps, and any other Forever addon's tracker sections) sit right under DaylightUI's quest tracker, follow it as it grows, moves and fades, and take its look: heads like its Quests header, names like quest titles, lines like objectives, from your Quests settings. A switch on its page puts them back where ForeverCore places them.
- OpenMap: the quill's "Draw on the map" tray is shrunk to fit DaylightUI's narrower world map instead of running past both sides.
- OneForAll: a "Hide OneForAll's world map button" switch on its page (put back when turned off).
- Shortest Path Forever no longer brings the game's waypoint icon, and its own pin, back under DaylightUI's in-world marker.
- New: DaylightUI Override. What OneForAll, Questie, Shortest Path Forever and Dialogue UI do that DaylightUI already does (OneForAll's chat hide button that hid DaylightUI's chat, its copy buttons, arrow-key history, minimap button collector, map pins, flight timer, loot feed, camera zoom, window moving and more; Questie's nameplate icons, tooltip lines, sound and auto quests; Shortest Path's map pins, destination pin and arrow; Dialogue UI's auto turn-in, book window and reward tooltip) is DaylightUI's: a window on first load lists each, DaylightUI picked, and you choose. Each addon's DaylightUI tab has a switch per feature and "Give everything back"; "Show the choices again" is on the All windows page.
- Lighter on play: the docked Forever sections only do work when the tracker moves, grows or fades; OneForAll's recolouring only runs after a click in its own window; settings are read once and kept until they change.
